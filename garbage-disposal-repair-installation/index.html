Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation in Tempe, AZ

A disposal that hums but won't spin, or one that's leaking from underneath, are two different problems with two different fixes.

Garbage disposal repair covers jams, leaks, electrical failures, and full replacement when a unit's motor has burned out or the unit is simply old enough to be unreliable. Most jams are a quick fix; leaks and electrical issues need a bit more diagnosis before we recommend repair versus replacement.

In North Tempe's rental-heavy, multi-tenant kitchens near ASU, disposals see heavier and more varied use than in a typical single-family home, which is part of why jams and premature wear show up more often in that part of our service area.

Common Problems

What We Fix

Humming But Not Spinning

Usually a jam against the impeller, often clearable without a full motor replacement.

Leaking From The Bottom

Often a failed seal, which typically means the unit needs replacement rather than a seal-only repair on most models.

Not Turning On At All

Could be a tripped reset button, an electrical issue, or a genuinely dead motor, each needing a different fix.

Foul Odor

Usually trapped food debris rather than a mechanical problem, though persistent odor after cleaning is worth a real inspection.

Repair or Replace

How We Decide

Jam vs. Motor Failure

A jam is almost always worth clearing rather than replacing the unit; a burned-out motor is not repairable.

Age Of Unit

A disposal past 8 to 10 years old with a new problem is often a better replacement candidate than another repair.

Leak Location

A leak from the sink flange is a different, often simpler fix than a leak from the unit body itself.

Noise At Startup

Grinding or rattling at startup that isn't from an object caught inside can indicate bearing wear worth factoring into the repair-versus-replace call.

Reset Button First

Many disposals have a reset button on the underside that trips on overload; checking that before assuming the worst is always the first step.
