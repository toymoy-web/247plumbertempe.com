Hydro Jetting in Tempe, AZ

When a line keeps clogging in the same spot, cabling it again usually just punches through the same buildup one more time.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water, not a mechanical cable, to scour the full interior wall of a pipe clean. It's the right tool for a line that's built up heavy scale, grease, or root intrusion over years, where a cable would just punch a channel through the blockage and leave most of the buildup in place to close back in.

In Tempe, the two most common reasons a line needs jetting rather than cabling are hard water scale, which narrows pipe diameter gradually over years, and root intrusion. Root intrusion shows up more in older North Tempe streets with mature trees planted near the sewer line's path.

When Jetting Is the Right Call

Jetting vs. a Simple Cable

Recurring Clogs

A line that clogs again within weeks of being cabled usually has buildup along the pipe wall that cabling alone won't remove.

Root Intrusion

Cabling can cut through roots temporarily, but jetting clears the intrusion point more completely and delays regrowth longer.

Grease Buildup

Kitchen line grease buildup, common in rental-heavy multi-tenant properties, coats the pipe wall in a way jetting removes and cabling doesn't.

Commercial & Multi-Unit Lines

Larger diameter commercial and multi-unit lines often need jetting's higher volume clearing rather than a residential-scale cable.

Process

How We Jet a Line Safely

Camera First

We inspect the line's actual condition before jetting, since a genuinely fragile or already-cracked pipe needs a different approach.

Pressure Matched to Pipe

Jetting pressure is set based on the pipe's material and condition, not a single default setting for every line.

Full Wall Clean

The jet scours the entire interior surface, not just a path through the blockage.

Camera Verify

A follow-up camera pass confirms the line is genuinely clear, not just flowing again temporarily.

Decision Framework

Hydro Jetting vs. Cabling

Cabling / Snaking

  • Faster and less expensive for an isolated, soft clog
  • Punches a channel through the blockage
  • A reasonable fit for a one-time object or hair clog
  • Often not enough for scale, grease buildup, or roots

Hydro Jetting

  • Scours the full pipe wall clean, not just a channel
  • The right call for recurring clogs or root intrusion
  • Removes grease buildup that cabling leaves behind
  • Costs more upfront but resets the line rather than patching it
