Geography

Tempe's Southern Neighbor, From Downtown To Ocotillo

Chandler has about 276,000 residents in the 2020 census and borders Tempe along the south side. Dr. A.J. Chandler founded the townsite in 1912, and the blocks around Dr. A.J. Chandler Park in historic downtown hold the oldest homes and storefronts in the city. Our separate South Chandler page covers the Kyrene Corridor along the Tempe border, and this page covers the rest of the city.

Away from downtown, Chandler grew in large waves. Master-planned communities like Ocotillo, with its lakes and golf, brought homes from the 1990s onward. The area around Chandler Fashion Center and the Intel Ocotillo campus drew apartments, offices, and retail, so Chandler is part older town, part newer suburb, and part business district.

Offices, restaurants, and retail around Chandler Fashion Center and the Intel campus mean commercial plumbing, grease trap service, and annual backflow testing come up here more than in a purely residential suburb. Residential calls lean toward water heater replacement in the older central blocks and softener, filtration, and tankless work in the newer southern neighborhoods.

We cover every Chandler ZIP code beyond the two on our South Chandler page, including 85225, 85286, 85248, and 85249, and every job gets the same flat, confirmed-before-we-start pricing. Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Chandler

Shut Off The Main Valve

If you know where your main water shutoff is, turning it off immediately stops the flow while help is on the way.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet, panel, or plugged-in appliance, shutting off power to that area at the breaker is a real safety step.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move furniture, electronics, and belongings out of the affected space if it's safe to do so, to limit further damage.
