Geography

Central And East Phoenix, Next Door To Tempe

Phoenix is the fifth-largest city in the country, with about 1.6 million residents in the 2020 census, and it borders Tempe along the north and west. Because the city is so large, this page focuses on the central and east side closest to us, including the Sky Harbor area, Papago Park, Arcadia, and the neighborhoods north of the Salt River. Ahwatukee, which is also part of Phoenix, has its own page.

ZIP 85034 surrounds Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport, which opened in 1928 and sits right along Tempe's northwest edge. ZIP 85008 runs from Papago Park, home to the Phoenix Zoo and the Desert Botanical Garden, east toward the Scottsdale border. Older apartments, ranch homes, and commercial properties fill much of this part of East Phoenix.

ZIP 85018 covers Arcadia, where many homes date from the mid-1900s and sit on large lots with mature trees. Mature trees and long sewer laterals are a recipe for root intrusion, and many Arcadia properties still use flood irrigation, so irrigation valves and lines are part of the plumbing picture.

ZIP 85040 covers older south Phoenix, from the I-10 corridor south toward Baseline Road, between downtown Phoenix, Tempe, and Ahwatukee. We cover all four of these ZIPs, 85008, 85034, 85018, and 85040, and every job gets the same flat, confirmed-before-we-start pricing. Drain cleaning and water heater repair are the services we are asked about most here.

Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Phoenix

Shut Off The Main Valve

If you know where your main water shutoff is, turning it off immediately stops the flow while help is on the way.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet, panel, or plugged-in appliance, shutting off power to that area at the breaker is a real safety step.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move furniture, electronics, and belongings out of the affected space if it's safe to do so, to limit further damage.
