Toilet Repair & Installation in Tempe, AZ

A toilet that's running, rocking, or leaking at the base is rarely worth ignoring in a rental-heavy market like North Tempe, where it just gets worse between tenants.

Toilet repair covers the everyday failures, a running fill valve, a weak or incomplete flush, a wobble at the base, a slow leak where the toilet meets the floor, plus full replacement when a unit is cracked, outdated, or simply due for an upgrade. Most of these are straightforward, same-visit fixes once the actual cause is identified.

In North Tempe's rental-heavy blocks near ASU, a running toilet or a loose wax ring often goes unreported for a while between tenants. That delay turns a five-minute fix into a bigger water-bill problem, or in the case of a base leak, real subfloor damage if it's caught late.

Common Problems

What We Fix

Running Toilet

Usually a worn flapper or fill valve, an inexpensive fix that stops a real, ongoing water bill drain.

Leak At The Base

Often a failed wax ring seal, which needs the toilet pulled and reset properly, not just caulked over.

Weak Flush

Can be a clog, a worn flush valve, or mineral buildup in the rim jets from hard water, each with a different fix.

Rocking Or Unstable Toilet

A toilet that rocks is often sitting on a damaged or uneven flange, which needs to be addressed before it causes a leak.

Installation

Replacement Done Right

Proper Wax Ring Seal

A correctly set wax ring is the difference between a toilet that seals for years and one that leaks within months.

Flange Condition Checked

We check the flange itself, not just the toilet, since a damaged flange will undermine even a brand-new installation.

Water-Efficient Options

Newer toilets use significantly less water per flush, which matters both for the bill and for reducing hard-water mineral flow through fixtures.

Haul-Away Included

The old unit is removed and disposed of as part of the installation.
