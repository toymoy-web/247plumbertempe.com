Drain cleaning in Tempe covers everything from a single slow bathroom sink to a main line backing up into a shower on the lowest floor of the house. Both start the same way here: something has narrowed the inside of the pipe until water can't move through fast enough, and the question is what that something actually is before anyone reaches for a cable or a jetter.

Tempe adds two real, local causes on top of the usual grease and hair. According to the City of Tempe's own water quality data, hardness averages 15 grains per gallon, and that mineral load deposits scale on the inside of pipe walls year after year. That's a slow, physical narrowing that has nothing to do with what a household puts down the drain. The second cause shows up seasonally. Tempe's monsoon season, roughly mid-June through the end of September, brings sudden, heavy storms that overwhelm older roof drains and area drains faster than most homeowners expect, and that's a real, separate spike in drain calls that has nothing to do with everyday buildup.

Common Causes

Why Tempe Drains Clog

Hard Water Scale

Tempe's 15 GPG average hardness deposits mineral scale inside pipe walls, narrowing the usable diameter over years.

Monsoon Overwhelm

Sudden June-through-September storms can overwhelm roof and area drains faster than a slow seasonal buildup would.

Rental Turnover

North Tempe and ASU-area rentals see grease, hair, and wipes buildup accumulate across multiple tenants before anyone calls it in.

Root Intrusion

Mature trees along older North Tempe streets send roots into aging clay sewer laterals through joints and small cracks.

Signs to Watch For

When It's More Than One Slow Drain

Multiple Slow Drains

If more than one fixture is slow at the same time, the blockage is usually further down the line, not at that one fixture.

Gurgling Sounds

Air trapped behind a partial blockage often gurgles back up through a nearby drain or toilet.

Backup After Storms

A drain that backs up specifically during or right after a monsoon storm points to the main line or an area drain, not the fixture itself.

Recurring Clogs

The same drain clogging again within weeks usually means the real cause, often root intrusion or heavy scale, was never actually cleared.

Decision Framework

Cabling vs. Hydro Jetting

Cabling / Snaking

  • Faster and less expensive for a single, soft clog
  • Punches through the blockage rather than removing it
  • A reasonable fit for an isolated hair or object clog
  • Often not enough against root intrusion or heavy scale

Hydro Jetting

  • High-pressure water scours the full pipe wall clean
  • Removes grease and scale buildup, not just a channel through it
  • The right call for recurring clogs or root intrusion
  • Costs more upfront, but resets the line rather than patching it
