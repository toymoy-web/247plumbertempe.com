Emergency Water Line Repair & Replacement in Tempe, AZ

The line between your water meter and your house is a different repair than anything inside the home, and expansive soil is exactly why it fails.

The water service line runs underground from the city's meter to the house, separate from both the interior plumbing a repipe would replace and the sewer line that carries wastewater the other direction. When this line fails, it typically shows up as a sudden drop in pressure, an unexplained wet area in the yard between the meter and the house, or a spike in the water bill from a leak that's running continuously underground.

According to the same soil pattern documented across the City of Tempe's service area, expansive clay and caliche soil is a real, physical cause of water line failures here. The same soil movement that cracks sewer lines and causes slab leaks also stresses a buried water service line over years of seasonal shifting.

Signs of a Failing Line

What to Watch For

Sudden Pressure Drop

A noticeable, sitewide pressure drop that isn't tied to a single fixture points to the main service line rather than interior plumbing.

Wet Area In The Yard

An unexplained soggy or unusually green patch of yard between the meter and the house is a classic sign of an underground leak.

Rising Water Bill

A leak in the service line runs continuously, which shows up as a steady, unexplained increase in usage.

Sound Of Running Water Near The Meter

Audible water flow at the meter box with everything in the house shut off points directly to the service line itself.

Repair Options

Trenchless vs. Traditional Replacement

Trenchless Replacement

A new line can sometimes be pulled through the old line's path with minimal digging, preserving landscaping and hardscape.

Traditional Trenching

A full trench is sometimes necessary depending on the line's condition, depth, and what's in the way above it.

Spot Repair

A single, localized failure point can sometimes be repaired directly without replacing the entire line.

Material Upgrade

Replacement is a chance to upgrade from an aging original material to modern, longer-lasting pipe.
