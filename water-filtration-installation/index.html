Water Filtration Installation in Tempe, AZ

Filtration and softening solve different problems, and knowing which one you actually need matters before spending money on either.

Water filtration targets taste, odor, and specific contaminants, which is a genuinely different job from a water softener, which targets hardness minerals specifically. The City of Tempe adds chlorine for disinfection, and while the water meets all federal and state quality standards, some households still prefer to filter out the taste that treatment leaves behind.

Options range from a simple under-sink filter for drinking and cooking water to a whole-home system that treats water at the point it enters the house. Which one makes sense depends on what you're actually trying to solve, taste at the tap, or broader water quality concerns across every fixture.

Filtration Options

Whole-Home vs. Under-Sink

Under-Sink Filtration

Treats water at a single point, typically the kitchen sink, for drinking and cooking without treating the whole house's supply.

Whole-Home Filtration

Treats water as it enters the home, affecting every fixture including showers, which some households prefer for skin and hair as well as taste.

Reverse Osmosis

A more thorough under-sink option that removes a broader range of dissolved substances than a standard carbon filter.

Combined With Softening

Filtration and softening can be installed together for households wanting both hardness reduction and taste improvement.
