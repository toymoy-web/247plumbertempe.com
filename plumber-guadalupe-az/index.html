Neighborhood Overview

Plumbing in Guadalupe, Arizona

Guadalupe is one of the smallest incorporated towns in Maricopa County, a tight 0.8 square miles bounded by Interstate 10, Baseline Road, the SRP Highline Canal, and Tempe on three sides. The whole town uses a single ZIP code, 85283, which it shares with part of South Tempe, but Guadalupe itself is a distinct, close-knit community centered on Avenida del Yaqui, its main commercial strip.

Most homes here were built between the 1970s and the 1990s, modest single-family houses on small lots, some with the wraparound porches and adobe-style details that give the town its own visual character. A lot of that housing stock is now well past the age where original plumbing, especially galvanized supply lines and aging water heaters, starts causing real problems.

Guadalupe's per-capita income runs below the county average, and a real, practical effect of that is fewer full remodels and more homes still running their original fixtures decades in. That's not a knock on the town, it's just a reason a straight, upfront price matters more here than in a neighborhood where everyone's already replumbed. In the 85283 ZIP, we see the most calls for water heater repair and drain cleaning specifically, which tracks with a housing stock this age.

We cover every real ZIP code in Guadalupe: 85283, and every job here gets the same flat, confirmed-before-we-start pricing as the rest of our service area.

Before We Arrive

If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in Guadalupe

Shut Off The Main Valve

If you know where your main water shutoff is, turning it off immediately stops the flow while help is on the way.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet, panel, or plugged-in appliance, shutting off power to that area at the breaker is a real safety step.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move furniture, electronics, and belongings out of the affected space if it's safe to do so, to limit further damage.
