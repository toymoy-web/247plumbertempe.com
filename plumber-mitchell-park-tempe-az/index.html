Neighborhood Overview

About Mitchell Park

Mitchell Park takes its name from the city park of the same name, and the City of Tempe treats Mitchell Park East and Mitchell Park West as named neighborhoods. Streets commonly associated with the area include West 13th Street, South Wilson Street, South Roosevelt Street, and South Mitchell Drive, all in ZIP code 85281. Published boundaries vary by source, so we describe it by those anchor streets.

Homes here are a real mix. Real estate listings for the neighborhood show single-family houses beside condos and townhomes, with construction years running from the mid-1940s to recent decades. In 2010 the city finished a traffic calming and streetscape project across Mitchell Park East and West, adding traffic circles and other features meant to slow cut-through traffic.

For plumbing, that mix matters more than any single era. A house, a townhome, and a remodeled bungalow on neighboring blocks can have different fixtures, water heaters, and shutoff arrangements, so we start every job by finding out what is actually there.

Mixed-Age Streets

What Mixed-Age Streets Mean For Plumbing

Different Homes, Different Setups

A freestanding house and a townhome can sit a block apart and share almost nothing in how they are plumbed. We identify the setup first, then recommend the repair.

Shared Walls And Shutoffs

In townhomes and condos, find out which shutoff valves serve your unit and what the association covers before a repair. We can help you locate the valves and document the problem.

Remodels And Additions

A kitchen or bath update can join new fixtures to older supply and drain lines. Those connections are a sensible place to look when a fixture leaks or loses pressure.

Owners And Landlords

A home can be owner-occupied one year and rented the next. We keep clear notes on what we found and what we did, so the next person to open the cabinet knows the history.
