Neighborhood Overview

About Hudson Manor

Hudson Manor is a planned postwar subdivision in east-central Tempe. City plat records held by the Tempe History Museum describe its units as bounded by Williams, Oak, and Cedar Streets and near the intersection of Orange, Howe, and Price Road. East Hudson Drive carries the neighborhood name, and addresses fall in ZIP code 85281.

The City of Tempe's own survey of post-World War II subdivisions lists Hudson Manor with construction from 1948 to 1955 and marks it as potentially eligible for historic status. The plat records for the later units run through 1958. Nearby named neighborhoods include University Heights, Alegre Community, and Jen Tilly Terrace.

A subdivision built over roughly a decade in one era tends to share floor plans, lot layouts, and system ages from house to house, though remodels change that quickly. We treat that as a starting point and then confirm what is actually installed in your home.

Plumbing In A Mid-Century Subdivision

Checked, Not Assumed

Homes from this period are now in the age range where water heaters, supply lines, and drain lines deserve a close look. We confirm the materials in your house instead of assuming them from the year it was built.

Leaks You Cannot See

Slow leaks inside walls or beneath floors often show up first as a higher water bill or a damp spot. Leak detection finds the source before anything is opened up.

Before You Repipe

If supply lines do need replacing, an inspection and a written scope come first. Age alone does not decide it, and we explain what we find before recommending anything.

Remodeled Homes

Many homes from this era have been updated over the years. We look at where newer plumbing meets the original lines, since those joints are a common place for trouble.
