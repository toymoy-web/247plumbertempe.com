A Newer Build-Out Than Its Tempe Neighbors

South Chandler's 85226 ZIP code, reached from Tempe along the Kyrene Corridor, grew primarily through master-planned communities like Andersen Springs, McQueen Lakes, and Twelve Oaks. That build-out came later than Guadalupe's 1970s-90s wave and is largely tied to the area's growth as a technology employment hub, with Intel's Chandler campus a short drive away.

That later build-out means fewer of the original-galvanized-pipe calls that show up in older Tempe neighborhoods. If You Have a Burst Pipe or Active Leak in South Chandler
