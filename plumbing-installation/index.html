New Plumbing Installation in Tempe, AZ

New plumbing installation is a different job than repair or replacement: it's building a system from scratch, not fixing one that's already there.

Plumbing installation covers new supply and drain lines for spaces that don't have them yet: an accessory dwelling unit, a room addition, a full kitchen or bathroom remodel adding new fixtures, or new construction. This is a rough-in and layout job at its core, running lines, setting drain slopes, and placing supply stubs before walls and flooring close in around them, distinct from repairing or replacing an existing system.

Arizona's 2024 statewide ADU law, House Bill 2720, requires Tempe and other cities over 75,000 residents to allow accessory dwelling units on single-family lots, and that's created real, steady demand for exactly this kind of ground-up plumbing work across our service area.

Common Installation Projects

What This Covers

ADU Plumbing

A detached or attached accessory dwelling unit needs its own full kitchen and bathroom rough-in, tied into the main line or a new service connection.

Room Additions

Extending a home's footprint means extending supply and drain lines to reach new fixtures in the addition.

Kitchen & Bath Remodels

A remodel that relocates a sink, adds a second bathroom, or moves a fixture's position needs new rough-in work, not just a fixture swap.

New Construction

A new build starts with a completely blank plumbing layout, planned and installed from the foundation up.

What's Involved

The Rough-In Process

Layout Planning

Supply and drain line routing is planned around the space's actual fixture layout before anything gets installed.

Rough-In Installation

Lines are run through walls, floors, and slabs while everything is still open and accessible.

Pressure & Drain Testing

The new system is pressure-tested and drain-tested before walls close in around it, since problems are far cheaper to catch now than after.

Permit & Inspection

New plumbing installation requires a permit and inspection from the City of Tempe, which we handle as part of the job.

Fixture Trim-Out

Once walls and flooring are finished, final fixtures, faucets, toilets, sinks, are set and connected as the last step.

What This Actually Costs

Every quote is confirmed in writing before we start. Real factors that affect price:

ScopeA single-bathroom ADU rough-in costs less than a full kitchen-and-bath ADU or a whole-home new build.

Fixture CountMore fixtures means more supply and drain runs, which scales the labor and material cost directly.
