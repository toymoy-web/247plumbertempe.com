Commercial plumbing covers restaurants, offices, retail, and multi-tenant properties, work that comes with its own priorities: minimizing downtime during business hours, meeting code requirements specific to commercial fixtures and grease management, and often working around a tenant's operating schedule rather than a homeowner's.

Tempe's Mill Avenue and ASU-area corridor has a real concentration of restaurants and food service businesses, which brings its own recurring commercial plumbing need: grease trap maintenance and drain line clearing for kitchens generating far more grease volume than a residential kitchen ever would.

Common Commercial Needs

What We Handle

Grease Trap Service

Restaurant kitchens need regular grease trap pumping and maintenance to avoid backups and stay compliant.

Commercial Drain Clearing

Higher-volume commercial drain lines see grease and debris buildup faster than residential lines and often need hydro jetting rather than simple cabling.

Backflow Testing

Many commercial properties have annual backflow testing requirements tied to their specific connections and equipment.

Multi-Fixture Repairs

Office and retail properties with multiple restrooms need repairs coordinated to minimize disruption to customers or staff.

Water Heater Systems

Commercial water heater needs, often larger or multiple units, differ from a single residential tank in both sizing and code requirements.

How We Work With Businesses

Minimizing Disruption

After-Hours Scheduling

Non-emergency work can often be scheduled outside business hours to avoid disrupting operations.

Property Manager Coordination

We're used to working with property managers and tenant coordination rather than dealing only with a single homeowner.

Code Compliance

Commercial fixtures and connections follow different code requirements than residential work, and we handle those specifics.

Documentation For Records

Commercial clients often need service documentation for their own records or lease compliance, which we provide.

Decision Framework

One-Off Service vs. Maintenance Contract

One-Off Emergency Calls

  • No commitment, pay per visit
  • Works fine for infrequent, unpredictable issues
  • Can cost more over a year than a maintenance plan
  • No priority scheduling during peak-demand periods

Maintenance Contract

  • Scheduled grease trap and drain service on a set interval
  • Priority scheduling for emergency calls
  • Often less expensive over a year than reactive-only service
  • A consistent point of contact who already knows the property
