Sewer Camera Inspection in Tempe, AZ

A camera inspection is the only way to actually see what's happening inside a sewer line, rather than guessing from symptoms alone.

A sewer camera inspection runs a waterproof camera through the line to show its real, current condition, root intrusion, cracks, bellied sections, or buildup, without any guesswork. It's used both as a diagnostic step before a repair and as a standalone service for homebuyers wanting to know a property's sewer line condition before closing.

In Tempe specifically, a camera inspection is worth considering for older homes given the area's expansive clay and caliche soil. That soil movement is a real, physical cause of the cracked joints and root intrusion that show up on inspection footage in older North Tempe and Guadalupe properties.

When to Get One

Real Reasons to Inspect

Before Buying A Home

A camera inspection before closing reveals the sewer line's actual condition, information a visual walkthrough can't provide.

Recurring Clogs

A line that clogs repeatedly despite cabling or jetting needs a camera to identify the actual structural cause.

Before Major Landscaping

Confirming a line's path and condition before planting trees or major hardscaping can prevent future root intrusion problems.

Insurance Or Warranty Documentation

Recorded footage provides real documentation for an insurance claim or a home warranty dispute.

What the Inspection Shows

What We're Actually Looking For

Root Intrusion

Camera footage shows exactly where roots have entered the line and how much they're restricting flow.

Cracks & Joint Separation

Structural damage from soil movement is visible directly, not inferred from symptoms.

Bellied Sections

A section of pipe that's sagged and collects standing water shows up clearly on camera, a common cause of recurring slow drainage.

Pipe Material & Condition

The camera confirms the actual pipe material, useful when deciding between a repair method and factoring the line's remaining service life.
