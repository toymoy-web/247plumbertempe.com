Faucet & Sink Repair in Tempe, AZ

A dripping faucet in Tempe usually means hard water has finally worn through an internal seal, not that the fixture is simply old.

Faucet and sink repair covers drips, leaks under the sink, low flow, and full fixture replacement across kitchens and bathrooms. Most drips trace back to a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring, straightforward parts that wear out faster in Tempe's hard water than they would elsewhere.

A leak under the sink is a separate issue from a dripping spout, usually a failed supply line connection or a worn P-trap seal. It's worth addressing quickly since cabinet and subfloor damage from a slow under-sink leak is easy to miss until it's already extensive.

Common Problems

What We Fix

Dripping Faucet

Almost always a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring, a quick fix once the faucet's internal type is identified.

Leak Under The Sink

Often a failed supply line connection or worn P-trap seal, distinct from a leak at the faucet itself.

Low Water Pressure At The Tap

Mineral buildup in the aerator or supply line is a common, simple cause worth checking before assuming a bigger problem.

Loose Or Wobbly Faucet

A faucet that's come loose from the sink deck needs to be reseated and secured before it causes a seal to fail.

Installation

Replacement Done Right

Proper Supply Line Connections

New, correctly sized supply lines are installed rather than reusing old ones that may already be worn.

Sealant Applied Correctly

A proper seal at the sink deck prevents the exact kind of slow leak that damages cabinets over time.

Shutoff Valves Checked

Old shutoff valves are tested and replaced if they don't fully close, since a valve that won't shut off makes future repairs harder.

Haul-Away Included

Old fixtures are removed and disposed of as part of the installation.
